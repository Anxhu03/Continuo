STEP 01

Download Continuo

Download the extension package to your computer.

Download Continuo Extension continuo-extension.zip
STEP 02

Open Chrome Extensions

Open Chrome's extension manager by navigating to chrome://extensions in your address bar.

chrome://extensions
STEP 03

Turn on Developer Mode (Enable Developer Mode)

Enable Developer mode so Chrome can load the development version. Turn on the toggle switch in the top-right corner of the Extensions page.

STEP 04

Extract Continuo

Open the downloaded ZIP and extract it to a folder on your computer.

STEP 05

Load Continuo

Click Load unpacked and select the extracted Continuo extension folder.

STEP 06

Pin Continuo

Pin Continuo to your Chrome toolbar so it's always available during AI conversations.

Workflow

WHAT HAPPENS NEXT?

Continuo works quietly in the background during your existing workflow.

01

Open ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini

Visit any of the supported AI platforms in Chrome where you normally brainstorm, code, or design.

02

Start or open a project conversation

Work normally on your task, discuss architectural decisions, solve bugs, or refine prompts.

03

Click Continuo and save your context

One click saves the conversation's essence to project memory, ready to hand off immediately.

Continuo takes care of the complicated part. The complexity stays inside the engine, not in the user interface.
